🌙 Eid Mubarak 🌙

Eid-al-Adha ko "Bakrid" bhi kehte hain. Ye Islam ka ek bada tyohar hai jo Hazrat Ibrahim (A.S) ki Allah ke liye qurbani ki yaad me manaya jata hai.

Is din Musalman log:

  • Subah Eid ki namaz padhte hain
  • Allah ke naam par janwar ki qurbani karte hain
  • Gharibon aur rishtedaron me gosht baantte hain
  • Naye kapde pehente hain aur ek dusre ko "Eid Mubarak" kehte hain

Allah aapki qurbani aur duaon ko qubool farmaye. Aapke ghar me hamesha khushi, barkat aur aman rahe. Eid Mubarak! 🤍🕊️
